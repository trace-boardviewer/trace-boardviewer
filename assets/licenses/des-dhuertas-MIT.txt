DES tables and round structure follow dhuertas/DES (https://github.com/dhuertas/DES),
"Data Encryption Standard - An approach to DES algorithm" by Daniel Huertas Gonzalez,
based on FIPS PUB 46-3. Used in TRACE only to decode legacy vendor boardview files.
