- Opening a board: the loading card now shows how far the reader got ("42% processed") for GenCAD and KiCad boards, which are the formats that reach hundreds of thousands of pins, and has a **Cancel** button for every format. Cancel stops the file reader at once, keeps the board that was open before, and says that opening was cancelled.
- A file that makes the reader stop progressing no longer leaves the loading card up for good. After 30 seconds without progress the card says the file is taking unusually long, and Cancel stays available. After two minutes without progress the reader is stopped and a message says so and that the file may be damaged or unusually complex. A reader that keeps reporting progress is never stopped. The new texts are in all eight languages.
