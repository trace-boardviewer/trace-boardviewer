BV2 text boardviews now import their complete Layout, Nail and Pin CSV sections through an original bounded reader. The shared BV mapping preserves inch coordinates, explicit sides, pin names, nets, test-point rows and outline order. Numeric Pin annotations may repeat while source Name remains the pin identity. Both available exports imported with every source scalar and board assignment checked: 5,394 components, 14,812 pins and 254 outline points, with zero mismatches.

Quoted fields and UTF-8, Windows-1252 or valid UTF-16 text are supported. Unknown schemas, malformed records, duplicate identities, unsupported outline groups and exceeded limits fail explicitly. MIL and NO_PROBE test-point annotations do not invent physical dimensions or probe availability; NO_PROBE electrical points are retained with a warning. Pads and component bodies use disclosed estimates, and non-zero outline radii use straight segments. The text export has no declared row counts or terminal marker. No sample files are distributed.
