- BVR boardviews: a pad that has neither a PIN_NUMBER nor a PIN_NAME (the fiducials, mounting holes and unconnected pads that kicad-boardview writes without a number) is now numbered "~" and its place in the part (~1, ~2, ...) instead of its place alone. It no longer shares its number with a real pad of the same part (a mounting hole and pad 1 were both "1"), and it is shown as a number the reader made up, like the unnumbered pads of KiCad boards.
