TVW now opens through an original MIT-reference reader. Full declared component tables and pin UID/8 links supply physical pad coordinates, nets, sides and dimensions, including test points and reordered pins. Compact, height-word and extra-Pascal metadata layouts are validated on real exports. Board outlines and copper traces are not imported; custom pad geometry uses bounding boxes. The pin list's layer reference is read as the index into the full list of layer headers, with the header type deciding the side, so exports with more aux, silk, mask or inner layers resolve correctly; net tables with a different closing word or prefix are found; AppleDouble companion files (`._` names) are refused with a clear message. Invalid tables, unknown layer numbers and layer-header variants that are not known fail explicitly with a precise message; no vendor samples are distributed.
