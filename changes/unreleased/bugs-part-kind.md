- Part kinds: the board canvas, the inspector, the search rows and the part icons now share one classifier, so they always agree (IC7 was a chip on the board and a "Capacitor" in the inspector). Integrated circuits written IC or PMIC are chips; connectors named CN, test points (TP, PP), resistor arrays (RN, RP), crystals and oscillators (OSC), ferrite beads (FB, or an inductor whose value is a ferrite impedance such as 600R@100MHz), LEDs, switches and jumpers each get their own label, and mounting holes, stand-offs, fiducials and logos are labelled "Mechanical part" instead of "Connector". A designator the classifier does not know is a plain "Component" instead of a guess from its last letter, and the package and value are used when the designator says nothing (an LED footprint under a name like BOOT1). Seven new labels exist in all eight interface languages.
